# Board & Base — Community Apps

A collection of little screens for your **Board & Base display** — the small pixel
screen that sits on your desk.

Each app shows something useful at a glance. Pick one from the list, add it to your
display, and you're done.

## Apps

| App | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [Claude Agents](apps/agents) | How many jobs your Claude assistant is running right now, and how many are finished. |
| [Claude Usage](apps/usage) | Your current Claude session usage as a percentage, with an animated graph and when your tokens run out vs. when the session resets. |

More apps are on the way.

## Adding an app to your display

1. Open an app from the list above and **download its file** (the one ending in `.star`).
2. **Paste it into your display's dashboard.**
3. Fill in the short **Setup** shown on the app's page (a box or two), then save.

That's it — it shows up on your display.

## Making your own

No coding needed — the dashboard does the hard part. In the **Custom apps** section of
your display's dashboard:

1. Click **Copy AI prompt** and paste it into a free chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini).
2. Tell it, in plain English, what you'd like your app to show.
3. Paste the code it gives you into the box and click **Test** to see a preview. If
   something looks off, click **Copy error** and paste that back to the chatbot to fix it.
4. Give it a name, fill in any settings, and click **Save & add to rotation**.

Made something nice? Share it here so others can use it too.
